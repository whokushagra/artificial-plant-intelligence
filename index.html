What we take in shapes us.

Today, more and more of it arrives through a screen.

Just having your phone on the desk lowers the working memory you have available, even when it is face-down and silent.Ward et al. 2017 · [5]
Same brain · different input

Change the input, change the pattern.

A day of screens
Higher screen use in early childhood is linked to weaker white-matter in language and reading pathways.Hutton et al. 2020 · [4]
Constant notifications split attention and may reshape how we focus and remember.Firth et al. 2019 · [6]
An hour in nature
After a one-hour walk in nature, activity in the amygdala, the brain's stress centre, goes down.Sudimac, Sale & Kühn 2022 · [2]
A nature walk quiets rumination and the region tied to it. Attention scores rise by about 20%.Bratman 2015 · Berman 2008 · [1][3]

Change what we take in, and we change who we become.

Two hours a week in nature is linked to better health and wellbeing [7]. We believe a brain that notices living things grows more connected, and more caring.

Artificial Plant Intelligence

A plant with a mind people can understand, and one that learns to understand you.

How it thinks.

01
Sense

It feels its world. We look inside the plant: every sense becomes a line of light.

Five live channels · light · soil moisture · air & root temperature · humidity
02
Signal

Many feelings braid into one signal, like nerves into a spine.

Edge microcontroller · streams telemetry every 2 s
03
The seed

Everything flows into its mind. Not a box, not a human brain. A seed.

The plant's own brain · where every step below happens
04
Memory

It remembers. Every reading is written down and kept.

Continuous logging · a living timeline of its days
05
Judge

It makes sense of each feeling, one sense at a time.

Neural classifiers · one trained judge per sense, taught on plant-science ranges
06
World

It knows its place in time: day or night, the weather, the season.

Context engine · time of day · weather · seasonal rhythm
07
Decide

Like your prefrontal cortex, it weighs it all and decides how it feels. If it isn't sure, it says so.

Decision cortex · admits doubt below 70% confidence
08
Personality

It stays itself. It knows what it is, feels only what is real, and never pretends.

Species knowledge + live data + a character that cannot break
09
Voice

It finds the words, and listens back.

On-device language model · speech in, voice out · fully local
10
And it talks back.

The seed becomes a face.
